KNOWLEDGE / 05

Learning studio

Short explanations to help you understand molecular chemistry.

01 / BASICS

What is a molecule?

A molecule is a group of two or more atoms held together by chemical bonds. Water (H₂O) and oxygen (O₂) are examples.

02 / STRUCTURE

Chemical formula

A chemical formula uses element symbols and numbers to show which atoms, and how many of each atom, are present in a molecule.

03 / BONDING

Covalent bond

A covalent bond forms when atoms share one or more pairs of electrons. Many common molecules contain covalent bonds.

04 / 3D CHEMISTRY

Molecular geometry

Molecular geometry describes the three-dimensional arrangement of atoms. Electron-pair repulsion helps explain many molecular shapes.

About MoleculeLab

A student-friendly space for exploring the building blocks of matter.

✳

Molecular Universe

MoleculeLab is an educational front-end project designed to make basic molecular information easier to explore. It includes a sample molecule library, property comparison, learning notes and an optional 3D structure viewer.

Molecular information is provided for learning and demonstration. Always consult reliable chemistry references for academic or laboratory work.
